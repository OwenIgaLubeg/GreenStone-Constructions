Our Standards

Class A & Class B+ Facilities

We categorize our developments to ensure transparency and deliver exactly the level of finish and functionality our clients require.

Class A Facilities

Our premium tier, designed for top-tier corporate, professional, and medical clients seeking impressive, state-of-the-art environments.

  • Impressive atriums with marble flooring and custom finishes
  • Zoned HVAC systems (optimized for every 800–1,500 sq. ft.)
  • Computerized security and advanced lighting systems
  • Deep-cell parabolic lighting to reduce glare and eye strain
  • Premium amenities: workout rooms, lockers, and concierge areas (on larger sites)

Class B+ Facilities

Our value-driven tier, perfect for businesses seeking solid, functional, and highly reliable spaces without compromising on service quality.

  • Strategically renovated older buildings brought to Greenstone standards
  • Solid, functional office or living spaces optimized for productivity
  • Upgraded electrical, plumbing, and security infrastructure
  • Modern aesthetic improvements and fresh finishes
  • Access to the same high-quality 24/7 property management services
