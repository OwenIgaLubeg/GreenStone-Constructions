Our Story

A Decade of Excellence in Property Development

Greenstone Property Development Ltd is a Lagos-based real estate development, construction, and facility management company. For over a decade, we have consistently delivered premium office and residential facilities tailored for corporate, professional, educational, and medical clients at highly competitive prices.

Our business model is uniquely built on long-term relationships, evidenced by our industry-leading repeat tenants and lease renewals. With fully integrated in-house design, build, lease, and management capabilities, we ensure seamless execution from the initial blueprint to the final handover and beyond.

Our Vision

"To be the leading provider of high-end facilities in estate development in Nigeria and Africa."
